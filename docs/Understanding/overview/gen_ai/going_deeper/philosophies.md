
??? note "[Language Models Are Multiverse Generators](https://generative.ink/posts/language-models-are-multiverse-generators/)"
    A high-level way of thinking about LLMs: rather than collapsing to one deterministic output, a model generates a probability distribution over many possible continuations at once, a "multiverse" of potential ideas that a human can explore rather than being handed a single answer.
